About TextGenetic

Science you can ship

TextGenetic AI Inc. is a federally incorporated Canadian company based in Kitchener, Ontario, at the centre of the country's technology corridor. We were founded by engineers and data scientists with research backgrounds, and we build AI the way we'd want it built for our own families: measured, reviewable and private by design.

What we do

We work on two connected fronts. The first is workflow automation for specialized care — replacing lists, paper and memory with one coordinated view of patients, programs and reassessments. The second is neuroanalytics — turning clinical signals into neural biomarkers and progression analytics for neurodegenerative disease.

How we got here

Our predictive engine was validated first where mistakes are expensive: a municipal fleet. The City of Kitchener pilot ($100,000, Pitch Kitchener) proved the technology in live operations, and the Government of Canada selected it for the ISC Testing Stream. We then applied the same discipline to brain health.

Principles

  • Clinician in the loop. AI assists; a qualified person always makes the clinical call.
  • Privacy by design. De-identified, governed data foundations ready for research and audit.
  • Production first. Models that hold up outside the lab, in the tools people already use.
