ChartDesk: AI for paper records in long-term care

Reads the faxes, scans and handwritten pages a long-term care home receives, files each to the right resident, and gives nurses and physicians answers with their source. Built from three days of on-site discovery; tested on 377 realistic page images, with every lab value read correctly.
