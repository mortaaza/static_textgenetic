Credentials

Selected for the ISC Testing Stream

Innovative Solutions Canada (ISC) is a Government of Canada program that buys and tests pre-commercial Canadian innovations in real operational environments. TextGenetic AI has been selected for its Testing Stream.

What the Testing Stream is

The ISC Testing Stream evaluates late-stage innovations — Technology Readiness Levels 7 to 9 — through federal R&D contracts. A federal department tests the innovation in its own operations and reports on how it performed. Successful testing opens a direct pathway to commercialization with the federal government.

Why it matters

  • Independent validation. The evidence that our AI performs comes from a federal evaluator, not from us.
  • Real operations, not a lab. Testing happens in live settings with real data and real users.
  • Late-stage maturity. Selection confirms the technology is deployable, not a prototype.

Where the technology was proven first

Our predictive-AI platform earned its field record in the City of Kitchener fleet pilot. That track record, and the discipline behind it, is what we bring to brain-health neuroanalytics.
