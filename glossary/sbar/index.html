Glossary

SBAR

Situation, Background, Assessment, Recommendation: a standard structure nurses use to tell a physician about a change in a patient's condition.

SBAR is a short, structured way to communicate a clinical concern, widely used in hospitals and long-term care. The Situation says what is happening now; the Background gives the relevant history, medications and recent results; the Assessment is the nurse's view of what is going on; and the Recommendation (or Request) states what the nurse is asking for.

A good SBAR depends on having the right facts at hand: the latest lab results, recent hospital notes and the resident's goals of care. When those live on paper, assembling them during an evening call is slow and error-prone.
