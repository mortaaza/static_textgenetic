Glossary

Goals of care

A resident's documented wishes and agreed plan for the kind of care they want, including whether a hospital transfer fits those wishes.

Goals of care describe what matters most to a person and the level of treatment they want, agreed through conversation with the person or their substitute decision-maker and the care team. In long-term care, they often guide decisions such as whether to treat a problem in the home or transfer the resident to hospital.

These wishes only help if they are visible at the moment a decision is made. Surfacing them first, before the clinical details, keeps urgent decisions aligned with what the resident wants.

Why it matters to our work

When a resident's condition changes, ChartDesk shows goals of care at the top of the SBAR draft, to help avoid transfers the resident would not want.
