Glossary

Predictive maintenance

Using data and machine learning to forecast when equipment is likely to fail, so maintenance happens before the failure instead of after it or on a fixed schedule.

Traditional maintenance is either reactive (fix it when it breaks) or preventive (service it every N kilometres or months, whether it needs it or not). Predictive maintenance uses the equipment's own history — work orders, sensor and telematics data, usage patterns — to estimate the likelihood of a specific failure in a given window, and schedules work accordingly.

The benefits are fewer unexpected breakdowns, less downtime, longer asset life and lower cost, because healthy assets are not over-serviced and failing ones are caught early. The hard parts are data quality (work orders are messy), choosing a prediction target that crews can act on, and earning trust by being right in a way operators can verify.
